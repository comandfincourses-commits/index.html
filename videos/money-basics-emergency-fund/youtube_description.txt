Same ₹50,000 salary. Same city. Same lifestyle. One person can survive 6 months without income; the other runs out in 10 days. What's the difference? 💰

In this video, CA Ankit Bajaj explains the money basics nobody taught us: what money really is, why earning ≠ wealth, where your salary actually goes, and how an emergency fund buys you the most valuable thing in a crisis: TIME.

✅ What You Will Learn
• Why earning money and becoming wealthy are two different things
• ₹1,000 vs ₹15,000 saved a month = ₹1,68,000 difference in one year
• Where your ₹50,000 salary goes, and how money circulates in the economy
• Rule 1: Save before you spend (₹10,000/month = ₹1,20,000 a year)
• Rule 2: Small expenses add up (₹200/day ≈ ₹72,000 a year)
• How much emergency fund you need: 3, 6 or 12 months of essential expenses
• How to build it from ₹5,000 a month, and what to do if an emergency hits
