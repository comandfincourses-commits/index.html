₹1.2 crore ka ghar… ya ₹2.03 crore ka commitment? 🏠

Ghar kharidna sirf ek property decision nahi hai. Ye decide karta hai ki agle 15 saal tum kitni freedom ke saath jeeoge. Is video mein hum ek real-life example se samjhenge ki Renting vs Buying mein asli trade-off kya hai, aur EMI kaise chupchaap tumhare career aur life decisions control karne lagti hai.

📊 The Numbers (Example)
• Apartment price: ₹1.2 crore
• Down payment: ₹20 lakh | Home loan: ₹1 crore @ 9% for 15 years
• Monthly EMI: ₹1,01,427
• Total repayment: ≈ ₹1.83 crore (≈ ₹82.6 lakh sirf interest)
• Total outflow incl. down payment: ≈ ₹2.03 crore
  (stamp duty, registration, maintenance aur property tax alag)
• Same flat on rent: ₹35,000/month → ≈ ₹66,400 per month ka difference
