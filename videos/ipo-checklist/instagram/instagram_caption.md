## Caption
Paytm IPO: ₹2,150 issue price. -27% on Day 1. 📉

Lakhs of investors applied without checking a few simple things.
Here's the 7-point checklist I use before applying for ANY IPO 👇

1️⃣ Where is the money going? (Fresh issue vs OFS)
2️⃣ Are the numbers real? (Cash flow > profit)
3️⃣ Is the price right? (P/E vs peers)
4️⃣ Who is behind it? (Risk factors + litigation)
5️⃣ Who else is buying? (Anchors + QIB demand)
6️⃣ Don't trust GMP blindly
7️⃣ Plan your goal (long term vs listing gain)

Can't answer these? Don't apply. There will always be another IPO.
