In November 2021, lakhs of investors applied for Paytm, then India's biggest IPO, at ₹2,150. On listing day it closed about 27% lower. Most had skipped a few simple checks before clicking "Apply".

In this video, CA Ankit Bajaj gives you a practical 7-point checklist to run before applying for ANY IPO, the same questions a professional analyst asks.

✅ The 7-Point IPO Checklist
1. Where is the money going? Fresh issue vs Offer for Sale (OFS)
2. Are the numbers real? Revenue, profits, operating cash flow and debt
3. Is the price right? P/E vs listed peers ("Basis for Offer Price")
4. Who is behind it? Risk factors, litigation, promoter background
5. Who else is buying? Anchor investors and QIB demand
6. The number you shouldn't trust: Grey Market Premium (GMP)
7. Plan your goal: long term vs listing gain, ₹2 lakh retail limit, lottery allotment
