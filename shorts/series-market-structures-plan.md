# Series Plan: Market Structures in 90 Seconds

Animated Concept Story series (see `.claude/skills/comandfin-finance-shorts/references/visual-style-animated.md`). Each episode: 70–90s at 1.1x, one story world, one continuum dot highlighted, scorecard recap, and a CTA to the next episode.

| Ep | Topic | Story world (original, Indian) | Hook | Proof moment | Scene colour | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perfect Competition | Wheat mandi: Sharma, Kaur, Patel stalls | "Why can't this wheat seller charge even ₹1 more?" | +₹1 → every buyer leaves | Sky `#BFF0F7` | Script ready |
| 2 | Monopolistic Competition | Riya scrolling biryani outlets on a food app | "Same biryani. Same app. ₹350 vs ₹200?" | Premium dum biryani → profit → copycats | Teal `#77BDBB` | Script ready |
| 3 | Oligopoly | A highway with 3 telecom towers (fictional brands) | "Why do 3 companies raise prices in the same week?" | One cuts price → others follow → price war | Soft lavender | To write |
| 4 | Monopoly | Dark variant: one lamp-lit railway-station tea stall, the only seller | "One seller. No competition. What stops them charging ₹100 a chai?" | Price up, buyers have nowhere else to go; regulation steps in | Dark maroon `#1E0502` → bright | To write |
| 5 | All four compared | Continuum board + all four scorecards side by side | "4 market structures. 1 table. Save this." | Sliders move across all four | Blue `#0B6DB5` | To write |

**Publishing cadence:** one episode every 2–3 days, each pinned comment pointing to the next. Combine into one long-form YouTube video (~6 min) and a LinkedIn carousel (the scorecards) after Ep 5.
