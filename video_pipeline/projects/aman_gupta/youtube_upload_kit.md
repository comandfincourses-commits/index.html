## Description
What if the man behind one of India's most popular audio brands was once a chartered accountant who wasn't sure what he wanted to do with his life?

Meet Aman Gupta, co-founder of boAt.

🎓 Born and raised in Delhi. Studied at Delhi University and became a CA at a young age
🏦 Worked at Citibank, KPMG and Harman International
⚡ Tried entrepreneurship early. Co-founded Advanced Telemedia with his father and faced failures and uncertainty
📚 MBA from the Indian School of Business, with an international learning experience at Kellogg School of Management
🤝 2013: co-founded Imagine Marketing with Sameer Mehta
🎧 2016: launched boAt, with great sound, stylish design and affordable prices for young India
🦈 Became one of the most recognised faces on Shark Tank India

Lesson: you don't need everything figured out at the beginning. Sometimes your failures prepare you for the opportunity you haven't discovered yet.
