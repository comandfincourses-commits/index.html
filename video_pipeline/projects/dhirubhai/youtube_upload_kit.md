## Description
One room. One table. Three chairs. One telephone.
That's how Reliance started, and in FY26 it became the first Indian company to earn more than $10 billion in annual profit.

In this story you'll see how Dhirubhai Ambani went from a village school teacher's son in Chorwad to a petrol pump worker in Aden, then to a spice and yarn trader in Mumbai. You'll also see how he took Reliance public in 1977, survived the Licence Raj, a bear cartel and a stroke, and built the foundations of India's largest company.

Key lessons for founders, finance students and professionals:
- Start small, but start today
- Capital markets can turn ordinary people into owners
- Scale comes from backward integration: textiles → petrochemicals → refining
