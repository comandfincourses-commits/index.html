## Description
One room. One table. Three chairs. One telephone. That's how Reliance began.

Dhirubhai Ambani was a village school teacher's son from Chorwad who worked at a petrol pump in Aden. He started trading spices and yarn in Mumbai in 1958 and took Reliance public in 1977, turning lakhs of ordinary Indians into shareholders. He survived the Licence Raj, a bear cartel and a stroke, and kept building: textiles → petrochemicals → Jamnagar.

Today, Reliance is the first Indian company to earn more than $10 billion in annual profit (FY26: ₹95,754 crore net profit on ₹11.76 lakh crore revenue).
