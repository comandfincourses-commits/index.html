## Description
He started with just ₹5,000. He built a fortune of about ₹46,000 crore.

Rakesh Jhunjhunwala, India's Big Bull, grew up listening to his father, an income tax officer, talk about the stock market. His father had one condition: get qualified first. He became a Chartered Accountant in 1985 and then chose Dalal Street over a comfortable career.

📈 1986: 5,000 shares of Tata Tea at ~₹43 rose to ~₹143 in three months, a profit of nearly ₹5 lakh
⚡ Bold bets on Tata Power and Sesa Goa turned lakhs into crores
⌚ Titan, bought in the early 2000s, became one of the biggest wealth creators in his portfolio
💰 Forbes (2022) estimated his net worth at about $5.8 billion

His philosophy: study businesses, trust your conviction, think long term.

You don't need millions to build wealth. You need knowledge, courage, patience and the conviction to stay invested when others lose faith.
