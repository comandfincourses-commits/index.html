# Warren Buffett Series: Character Bible (realistic style)

**How to use with ChatGPT:** create the characters in the order below, in **one chat**, so ChatGPT keeps the faces consistent. Make **Boy 11** first, then ask for the other ages as "the same boy" in that chat. Don't use real names in prompts; the descriptions are enough.

**Common ending for every prompt** (already included below):
> Ultra-realistic live-action photograph, real person, natural skin texture with pores and fine imperfections, realistic hair strands, shot on a cinema camera with a 50mm lens, soft natural light, plain soft-grey studio background, waist-up, facing the camera, 16:9, clean full-frame image with no borders, no text, not CGI, not cartoon, not illustration, not airbrushed.

## Consistency checklist
- Boy 6, Boy 11, Teen 14 and Young Man 20 should share **hair colour, ear shape, eye colour and face shape**.
- Father should look like the boy's father (similar face shape).
- **Plain grey background and waist-up** for every portrait. Scenes are created later in the video tool.
- Download each at the highest resolution and name the files exactly as the headings (Boy 11, Boy 6 …). Then upload them to Flow as characters.
