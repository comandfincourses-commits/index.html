# Short-Form Formats for Finance Education

Pick one format per short. Each skeleton is for ~75s at 1.1x (~205 words). Scale beats proportionally.

## 1. Myth vs Fact
- Hook: "Everyone says <myth>. That's wrong."
- Beats: Myth → why people believe it → Fact with a number → real example → takeaway.
- Example: "Higher profit means more cash." Then the fact that profit ≠ cash flow, with working capital shown through a fast-growing D2C brand.

## 2. Three Mistakes
- Hook: "3 mistakes that kill your <financial model / interview / CMA prep>."
- Beats: Mistake 1 (quick), Mistake 2 (quick), Mistake 3 (the costliest, held back to keep the open loop) → fix → CTA.
- Example: Hardcoding inside formulas, no balance-sheet check, circular interest without a toggle.

## 3. Explain Like a CFO
- Hook: "If your CFO asks '<question>', here's the 20-second answer."
- Beats: Plain-English definition → formula → one-company example → how the CFO actually uses it.
- Example: EBITDA vs PAT, or why a CFO cares about Days Sales Outstanding.

## 4. Real Company Case (Number Breakdown)
- Hook: a striking verified number. "Zomato made ₹X but its cash flow told a different story."
- Beats: The number → what it means → the metric behind it → lesson for analysts.
- Always cite the annual report/quarter in edit notes.

## 5. Interview Question
- Hook: "Investment banking interview question: <question>. Most freshers get it wrong."
- Beats: Question → common wrong answer → right answer with logic → bonus follow-up question.
- Example: "Walk me through how ₹10 of depreciation flows through the 3 statements."

## 6. Excel / Power BI / AI Hack
- Hook: "Stop doing <slow method>. Do this instead."
- Beats: Pain → screen recording of the function (XLOOKUP, LET, LAMBDA, dynamic arrays, Copilot prompt) → result → where it's used at work.
- Visual: zoomed screen capture, cursor highlight, keystroke overlay.

## 7. Concept in 60 Seconds
- Hook: "<Concept> explained in 60 seconds. No jargon."
- Beats: Analogy (chai stall, EMI, rent) → formal definition → formula → example → interview one-liner.
- Example: WACC, DCF, Ind AS 116 leases, Expected Credit Loss.

## 8. Career Roadmap
- Hook: "Want to become a <financial analyst / equity research associate> in 2026? Here's the exact path."
- Beats: Skills (3) → tools → certifications → portfolio project → realistic salary range with source.
- Avoid guarantees; use ranges from credible salary surveys.

## Visual style defaults
- Speaker framed chest-up, upper third clear for on-screen text.
- Brand colours for text pops; max 6 words on screen at once.
- Charts: one simple bar or line, animated in, with the key number highlighted.
- Captions: bottom-centre safe area (avoid the bottom 15% and right 12% where platform UI sits).
