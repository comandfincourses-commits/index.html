# Visual Style: Animated Concept Story

Based on analysis of reference videos from the Federal Reserve Education / Econ Lowdown "Economics Explained" series (Perfect Competition, 6:18; Monopolistic Competition, 4:09). Use the **style and teaching structure** only. Never reuse their characters, shop names, music or logos. Create original Indian characters and settings.

## What makes the style work

1. **One recurring scene as the "world".** A market street with 3 named sellers (wheat stalls; pizza shops on a phone app). Every concept is shown happening in that world before it is named.
2. **Name the concept after showing it.** The story runs first, and then the term appears in bold ("Perfect Competition").
3. **The continuum graphic.** An awning-topped board: "Market Structure Continuum" with 4 dots: Perfect Competition → Monopolistic Competition → Oligopoly → Monopoly. It returns in every episode with the current dot highlighted. Later variant: "Pricing Power" with NO → MOST at the ends.
4. **Numbered feature cards.** A big numeral (1, 2, 3, 4) and a 4–6 word statement on white ("Easy for sellers to come and go"), revealed by a half-circle wipe while the story scene stays visible on the other half.
5. **Slider scorecard.** A white card titled with the market structure. Rows: Number of sellers (Few↔Many), Product differentiation (None↔A lot), Control over prices, Advertising, Ease of entry (Hard↔Easy). Each slider animates to its position as it's explained. This is the recap device.
6. **Simple graphs on a beige grid.** Demand/supply curves, the equilibrium dot, a horizontal firm demand line, an MC curve meeting the price line. One curve at a time, drawn on.
7. **The proof-by-story beat.** E.g. a seller raises price by $0.01 and every customer walks to the next stall ("SOLD OUT!" signs on the rivals). That's the moment the concept clicks.
8. **Thought bubbles** for abstract ideas (opportunity cost, a customer craving pizza).
9. **Pacing.** Few hard cuts (8–9 in 4–6 minutes). Motion comes from continuous morphs, zooms, wipes and pop-ins, so it feels calm and premium. In a Short, add a visual change every 2–4 s but keep the morph-based transitions.

## Palette (sampled)

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Sky / outdoor scenes | `#BFF0F7` |
| Graph grid / ground | `#E8D5B5` |
| Scorecard background | `#0B6DB5` (perfect competition), teal gradient `#77BDBB`→`#8ECDCF` (monopolistic) |
| Awning boards / headings | dark teal `#1E5B58` |
| Cards | white `#FFFFFF`, text near-black `#1A1A1A` |
| Curves | demand green `#1E6B4F`, supply/MC blue `#1F7AC0` |
| Accent dots | blue `#1F7AC0`, teal `#1A9A8A`, green `#1FA36B` |

Give each episode its own scene background colour (sky, teal, etc.) while cards, curves and the continuum stay the same, so the series reads as one set.

## Typography
Bold geometric sans (Montserrat / Poppins / Gilroy-style), headings 700–800 weight. Card statements 3 lines max, left-aligned.

## Vertical (9:16) adaptation for Shorts/Reels
- Story scene in the **top 55%**, card/graph/scorecard in the **bottom 45%** (replaces the left/right half-circle split).
- The continuum board sits at the top as a sticky header for 2–3 s when the structure is named.
- Keep everything inside the centre 1080×1350 area so the 4:5 crop for Instagram/LinkedIn works.
- Burnt-in captions sit just above the card area, not over the graph.

## Dark variant (for twist / problem beats)
Deep maroon-black room `#1E0502`, single hanging lamp (black shade, bulb `#E8B777`), faint wall shapes `#260B06`. Use for a monopoly, cartel, fraud or "what went wrong" beat, then wipe back to the bright world.

## Indian world ideas (original)
- **Mandi street:** wheat/onion/tomato sellers (e.g. "Sharma Gehu Bhandar", "Kaur Farm Fresh", "Patel Anaj") for perfect competition.
- **Food app on a phone:** biryani/momo/chai outlets with ratings, ₹ prices and "Bestseller" tags for monopolistic competition.
- **Highway with 3 petrol pumps / 3 telecom towers** for oligopoly.
- **A single railway station stall / city metro / power utility** for monopoly (dark variant works well here).

## Tools
Canva (animated stickers + Magic Animate), Adobe Express, After Effects, Vyond, Animaker, or CapCut with flat SVG assets. Export 1080×1920, 30 fps.
