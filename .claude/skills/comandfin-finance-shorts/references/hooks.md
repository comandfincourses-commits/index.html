# Hook Library (first 0–3 seconds)

A hook must be **under 12 words**, spoken in the first second, and mirrored as on-screen text.

## Formulas
| Type | Template | Finance example |
|---|---|---|
| Shocking number | "<Number> — and nobody talks about it." | "₹0 profit, ₹500 crore valuation. Here's how." |
| Contrarian | "<Common belief> is wrong." | "A high P/E doesn't mean a stock is expensive." |
| Direct question | "Do you know <question>?" | "Can a profitable company go bankrupt?" |
| Mistake warning | "Stop <doing X> in <context>." | "Stop using VLOOKUP in your financial models." |
| Interview trigger | "This question rejects 80% of candidates." *(only with a source; otherwise "most candidates")* | "Walk me through a DCF — in 60 seconds." |
| Relatable pain | "If <pain>, watch this." | "If your balance sheet never balances, watch this." |
| Curiosity gap | "The one ratio every CFO checks first." | — |
| Before/after | "From 2 hours to 2 minutes in Excel." | — |

## Hinglish variants
- "Profit hai, par cash nahi? Yeh dekho."
- "Interview mein yeh question 90% log galat karte hain." *(only if sourced; else "zyaadatar log")*
- "CA final ke students, yeh trick yaad rakhna."

## Banned openings
- "Hi guys, welcome back to my channel…"
- "In today's video we will discuss…"
- "Before we start, please subscribe…"
- Any logo sting or intro longer than 0.5 s
- Clickbait that the video doesn't deliver on
