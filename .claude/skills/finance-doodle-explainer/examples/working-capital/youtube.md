## Description
A company earns ₹10 lakh in profit, its sales cross ₹1 crore, customers love it and investors are impressed. A few months later, it cannot pay salaries or suppliers. How?

Cash flow problems are one of the biggest reasons businesses fail. Not because they are unprofitable, but because they run out of cash. Follow Arjun and his healthy-snacks business to understand one of the most important concepts in finance: working capital management.

What you'll learn
