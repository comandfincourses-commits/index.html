# Visual metaphor playbook: finance concepts → doodle shots

Each line gives a ready shot using the library. **[T]** = kinetic text card, **[C]** = character + props, **[D]** = data visual, **[S]** = split comparison. Combine 3–5 of them per concept so the visuals change with every beat.

## Personal finance
- **Salary / income**: [C] phone `header:"BANK"`, `big:"₹50,000"` slides up on a navy background · receipt "SALARY SLIP" with rows Basic/HRA/PF/TDS · character `armsUp` + cash_stack.
- **Taxes / TDS**: receipt rows with red deductions · hand prop takes coins away (`slide-right` out) · [T] "WHERE DID MY MONEY GO?" with `money` in red.
- **Budgeting (50/30/20)**: [D] pie [50,30,20] labelled Needs / Wants / Savings · three cards (box style) appearing in turn.
- **Emergency fund**: [C] umbrella over the character while the bg is `night` · vault · [T] "6 MONTHS OF EXPENSES".
- **Inflation**: [S] "2015 ₹100" with a full cart vs "2025 ₹100" with a half cart · counter showing a price rising · fire under cash on a desk.
- **Saving vs investing**: piggy_bank vs plant / chart_up · scale `tilt` towards investing.
- **Compounding**: coin → coins(3) → coins(8) with arrows · linechart exponential curve · money_tree `grow` · plant `size` 0.6 → 1.0 → 1.5 over three cuts.
- **SIP / rupee-cost averaging**: calendar "EVERY MONTH" + coin drops (`drop`) · bars of units bought (more units when the price is low).
- **Credit score (CIBIL)**: [D] gauge 300–900 · character with a pet-dog analogy (hand + label) · table: Payment history 35% / Utilisation 30% / Age 15%…
- **Credit card debt**: credit_card + fire · chain_ball "DEBT" next to a `tired` character · counter interest climbing to "36% p.a." in red.
- **Loans / EMI**: bank (`label: "BANK"`) with an arrow to the character · calendar "EMI DUE" · bars of principal vs interest over time.
- **Insurance**: shield / umbrella · [S] "₹25,000 premium" vs "₹10 lakh hospital bill" · hospital-style office label.
- **Retirement / NPS / PPF**: piggy_bank `label:"LOCKED TILL 60"` · old-hair character `armsUp` in a room · hourglass.

## Markets & investing
- **Stocks / ownership**: office prop `label:"TATA"`, then a small character with a slice of it (pie 1%) · [T] "YOU OWN A PIECE OF THE COMPANY".
- **Bull vs bear market**: [S] stock_screen up vs stock_screen down · rocket vs chart_down.
- **Diversification**: one basket → many coins spread out; [D] pie with 5 asset classes; [T] "DON'T PUT ALL YOUR EGGS IN ONE BASKET".
- **Risk vs return**: scale tilting · rocket (high return) vs shield (safety) · [D] bars FD 7% / Debt fund 8% / Equity 12%.
- **Mutual funds**: many characters (3, small `h:300`) put coins into one money_bag labelled with the fund manager character in a `suit`.
- **Index fund / Nifty 50**: table of the top 5 weights · [T] "BUY THE WHOLE MARKET".
- **Volatility**: linechart with a zig-zag `values` · character `shrug` · camera `shake`.
- **Dividend**: money_tree dropping a coin (`drop`) into the character's hands (`hold` pose).
- **IPO**: office + rocket · calendar "LISTING DAY" · counter for the share price.
- **Gold**: gold_bars + lock · bars of returns.

## Corporate finance & accounting (MBA / CA / CMA level)
- **Balance sheet**: [S] cards "ASSETS" | "LIABILITIES + EQUITY" · scale balanced (`tilt: 0`) · [T] "IT ALWAYS BALANCES".
- **P&L / income statement**: document `title:"P&L"` rows Revenue / COGS / EBITDA / PAT · bars waterfall-style (descending colours).
- **Cash flow vs profit**: [S] "PROFIT ₹10 Cr" document vs empty money_bag · character `confused` · [T] "PROFIT IS AN OPINION, CASH IS A FACT".
- **Working capital**: circle of arrows (cash → inventory → receivables → cash) using 3 curved arrows `bend: 0.3` + props (money_bag, briefcase, document).
- **Ratios (current, D/E, ROE)**: table with Formula / Value / Benchmark · gauge with custom segments (e.g. D/E 0–3).
- **NPV / time value of money**: [S] "₹100 TODAY" vs "₹100 IN 5 YEARS" (smaller note) · clock + coins · counter discounting down.
- **IRR**: linechart of NPV vs discount rate crossing zero · target prop.
- **Valuation / DCF**: magnifier over office · bars of 5 forecast years · arrow to a counter "₹1,250 Cr".
- **Comparable multiples (P/E)**: table of 4 peer companies with P/E in a column · scale.
- **Capital structure**: pie Debt vs Equity · chain_ball (debt) vs shield (equity buffer).
- **Audit / IND AS / IFRS**: document with `stamp:"APPROVED"` or `"QUALIFIED"` (red) · magnifier · character in a `suit` with `glasses`.
- **GST**: receipt rows CGST/SGST · arrow chain manufacturer → dealer → consumer (factory → office → cart).
- **Credit risk / default**: bank lending (hand + note) → character `tired` → cross prop · gauge "probability of default".
- **FP&A / budgeting vs actuals**: bars two colours per month (use 2 bars elements side by side or alternate colours) · [T] "VARIANCE".
- **Financial modelling**: calculator + document "MODEL.xlsx" · table of assumptions · gear (drivers).
