# Stick-Man Story Script: "Will AI Take Your Job?"

A six-scene doodle story for Google Veo. One hero, one robot, one journey: **worry → wonder → shock → doubt → heart → victory.**

---

## Meet the cast (paste these descriptions into every prompt, word for word)

**RAJU:** a cheerful young stick-figure finance professional with a round peach-coloured face, big expressive eyes behind round black glasses, a messy tuft of black hair, a bright sunflower-yellow shirt and a navy-blue tie that flaps when he moves.

**CHIP:** a small, bubble-shaped robot with a glossy teal-green body, a little screen for a face showing big cartoon eyes, short springy arms, and one bouncy antenna with a glowing coral-pink tip.

**LOOK:** colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with faint grid lines, bold navy outlines, vivid sunflower yellow, teal green, coral pink and sky blue, playful bouncy motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Scene 1: "Imagine a world where robots manufacture products…"
**Story:** Raju walks into a giant toy-coloured factory and freezes, mouth open. Dozens of robots are building everything in sight, and one of them, Chip, rolls up and hands him a perfectly made coffee mug.

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–3 s: Raju steps in, his yellow shirt glowing against a rainbow factory of spinning gears and conveyor belts.
- 3–6 s: Robots zip past, stacking teal and coral boxes; Raju's glasses slide down his nose.
- 6–8 s: Chip bounces up, offers a mug with a happy-face screen; Raju takes it, stunned.

**Veo prompt:**
> Colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with faint grid lines and bold navy outlines. RAJU, a cheerful young stick-figure finance professional with a round peach face, big eyes behind round black glasses, messy black hair, a bright sunflower-yellow shirt and a flapping navy tie, walks into an enormous candy-coloured factory and freezes in amazement. Teal, coral and sky-blue robots whizz along conveyor belts, spinning golden gears and stacking glossy boxes at lightning speed while tiny sparkles pop like confetti. His glasses slide down his nose. CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with a screen face showing big cartoon eyes and a bouncy antenna with a glowing coral-pink tip, rolls up happily and hands Raju a perfectly made coffee mug. Playful, bouncy, wonder-filled motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Scene 2: "AI manages businesses, autonomous cars drive you around"
**Story:** Raju steps outside into a city that runs itself. Chip, now wearing a tiny tie, sits in a glass office running a whole company on floating screens, then waves from a driverless car that glides past.

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–3 s: Chip in a mini tie juggles glowing pie charts and bar graphs that float around like balloons.
- 3–6 s: Raju presses his face to the office window, amazed; a coral driverless car rolls by with no one at the wheel.
- 6–8 s: A sky-blue delivery drone drops a parcel into Raju's hands; Chip waves from the car window.

**Veo prompt:**
> Colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with bold navy outlines. Inside a bright glass office, CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with a screen face of big cartoon eyes and a bouncy antenna with a glowing coral-pink tip, now wearing a tiny navy tie, happily juggles floating glowing pie charts and bar graphs in sunflower yellow and sky blue, running a whole business by itself. Outside, RAJU, a cheerful stick figure with a round peach face, round black glasses, messy black hair, a sunflower-yellow shirt and a navy tie, presses his face against the window in disbelief. A shiny coral-pink driverless car with an empty driver's seat glides past, and a sky-blue drone drops a parcel neatly into Raju's arms as Chip waves from the car. Busy, magical, upbeat city motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Scene 3: "Money itself could become almost meaningless"
**Story:** Raju proudly opens his wallet, but his coins and notes float away like soap bubbles. Robots fill the streets with free goods, and Raju's panic slowly melts into a confused smile.

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–3 s: Raju opens his wallet; golden coins and green notes start drifting upward.
- 3–6 s: He jumps to catch them as robots roll by with baskets overflowing with fruit, books, laptops and tiny houses.
- 6–8 s: Chip hands him an apple for free; Raju shrugs, smiles and takes a bite.

**Veo prompt:**

He leaps comically to grab them, tie flying, as a parade of teal and coral robots rolls past carrying baskets overflowing with red apples, stacks of books, glowing laptops and tiny colourful houses, an endless street of abundance. CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with big cartoon screen eyes and a bouncy coral-pink antenna, hands Raju a free red apple. Raju shrugs, smiles and takes a big bite. Whimsical, dreamy, funny motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no currency symbols, no logos.

## Scene 4: "Accounting, customer service, software, law, medicine and manufacturing could face disruption"
**Story:** Back at work, Raju looks around the office. At every desk (the accountant, the call-centre agent, the coder, the lawyer, the doctor, the factory worker) a robot quietly appears and starts copying their job. Then a robot sits down at Raju's own desk.

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–4 s: A colourful open-plan office; one by one, cheeky robots pop up with a "boing" beside six busy stick-figure professionals and mimic them.
- 4–6 s: Each human does a double-take, eyebrows shooting up.
- 6–8 s: Raju turns back to his own desk and finds Chip sitting in his chair, typing on his calculator; Raju gulps.

**Veo prompt:**
> Colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with bold navy outlines. A bright open-plan office with six busy stick-figure professionals in vivid outfits: an accountant with a calculator and ledgers, a customer-service agent in a headset, a coder at a glowing laptop, a lawyer holding balance scales, a doctor with a stethoscope, and a factory worker with a wrench. One by one, cheeky little teal, coral and sky-blue robots pop up with a springy bounce beside each person and copy their exact pose and task, and each human does a comic double-take with eyebrows flying up. RAJU, a stick figure with a round peach face, round black glasses, messy black hair, a sunflower-yellow shirt and a navy tie, turns to his own desk and finds CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with big cartoon screen eyes and a coral-pink antenna, sitting in his chair happily typing. Raju gulps nervously. Snappy, comic, slightly suspenseful motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Scene 5: "Would you want a robot to deliver the news of a life-threatening illness to your family?"
**Story:** Raju's family waits anxiously in a hospital corridor. A cold, shiny robot approaches with a clipboard, and everyone stiffens. Then a kind doctor steps in front of the robot, kneels, and gently holds the grandmother's hand. Even Chip nods: this job belongs to humans.

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–3 s: A soft-lit hospital corridor in pastel blues; Raju, his mother and grandmother sit close, holding hands.
- 3–5 s: A tall chrome robot with a blank screen-face rolls toward them; the family shrinks back.
- 5–8 s: A warm human doctor in a white coat steps forward, kneels and holds the grandmother's hand; the scene glows golden, and Chip, standing at the side, gives a small approving nod.

**Veo prompt:**
> Colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with bold navy outlines, softer and more emotional in tone. In a gentle pastel-blue hospital corridor, RAJU, a stick figure with a round peach face, round black glasses, messy black hair, a sunflower-yellow shirt and a navy tie, sits close to his mother in a coral saree and his grandmother in a lavender shawl, all holding hands nervously. A tall, cold, chrome-grey robot with a blank screen-face and a clipboard rolls toward them, and the family shrinks back with worried eyes. Then a kind human doctor in a white coat with a green stethoscope steps in front of the robot, kneels down and gently holds the grandmother's hand, and the whole corridor fills with a warm golden glow and tiny floating hearts. At the side, CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with big cartoon screen eyes and a coral-pink antenna, gives a small, respectful nod. Tender, slow, heartfelt motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Scene 6: "You won't lose your job to AI… you'll lose it to somebody who uses AI."
**Story:** The finale. Two colleagues get the same mountain of work: Rohan does it the old way and drowns in paper; Raju teams up with Chip and finishes in seconds. Raju zooms ahead on his office chair to a shining trophy, then turns to the camera with a wink, as if to say "your move".

**Beats (8 s):**
- 0–3 s: Split screen: two desks, two giant stacks of files land with a thud.
- 3–6 s: Left, Rohan (a stick figure in a grey shirt) sweats and types slowly as papers topple over him. Right, Raju high-fives Chip; files fly into neat glowing folders with green checkmarks and golden stars.
- 6–8 s: Raju zooms forward on his rolling chair like a race car, grabs a golden trophy, and winks at the camera; Chip's antenna flashes coral-pink.

**Veo prompt:**
> Colourful hand-drawn marker-and-crayon doodle animation on warm cream paper with bold navy outlines, energetic split-screen composition. Two matching office desks each receive a towering stack of files with a thud. On the left, ROHAN, a stick figure in a dull grey shirt, sweats and types slowly on an old keyboard while papers topple over him in a comic avalanche. On the right, RAJU, a cheerful stick figure with a round peach face, round black glasses, messy black hair, a bright sunflower-yellow shirt and a navy tie, high-fives CHIP, a small bubble-shaped glossy teal-green robot with big cartoon screen eyes and a bouncy antenna with a glowing coral-pink tip, and together they make the files fly into neat glowing folders as green checkmarks and golden stars burst into the air. Raju then zooms forward on his rolling office chair like a race car, leaving a trail of rainbow sparkles, grabs a shining golden trophy and winks at the camera while Chip's antenna flashes. Fast, triumphant, funny motion, vertical 9:16, no written words, no logos.

## Production tips for Google Veo / Flow
1. **Keep Raju and Chip consistent:** paste the cast descriptions word for word every time. In Google Flow, generate Raju and Chip once as still images, then use **Ingredients to Video** and attach both images to every scene.
2. **Generate 2–3 takes per scene** and keep the one where the faces and colours match best.
3. **8 seconds per clip** (Veo's standard length). I'll trim, slow down or extend each one to match your exact voice-over timing.
4. **Leave out words and logos.** AI video often misspells text, and my editor adds clean captions and the COMandFIN branding on top.
5. Upload all six MP4s here and I'll build the final Short.
