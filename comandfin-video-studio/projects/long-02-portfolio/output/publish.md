## Description
```
In 1985, Rakesh Jhunjhunwala walked into Dalal Street with just ₹5,000. Today, you can start investing with just ₹250. So what separates a beginner from a billionaire? Not luck. Not tips. A process. And that process is called a portfolio.

In this video, CA Ankit Bajaj takes you through 8 simple steps to build your own portfolio, with real lessons from Rakesh Jhunjhunwala (Titan), Warren Buffett (Coca-Cola), Radhakishan Damani (DMart) and Vijay Kedia (SMILE).

✅ THE 8 STEPS
1. Know yourself: goals, time horizon, risk, emergency fund & insurance
2. Start small: ₹250 SIP; ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% ≈ ₹50 lakh
3. Asset allocation: equity, debt & gold; core & satellite
4. Understand the economy: GDP, inflation, repo rate, crude, rupee
5. Choose the right sector: structural vs cyclical
6. Choose the right stocks: business, moat, management, numbers, price
7. Diversify sensibly: 10–15 stocks, max ~10% per stock
8. Review, rebalance & be patient
```
