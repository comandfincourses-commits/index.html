About the program

What is Financial Modelling?

A practical program that takes you from Excel fundamentals to a fully linked three-statement model, DCF valuation and comparable-company analysis on a listed company — following investment-banking conventions for structure, colour coding, error checks and scenario analysis.

Who should join

  • MBA, BBA and Commerce students targeting finance roles
  • CA / CFA / CMA aspirants and qualified professionals
  • Analysts in FP&A, credit, equity research and consulting
  • Founders and managers who work with business plans and valuations

What you will be able to do

  • Build a fully linked 3-statement model with no hard-coding
  • Value a company using DCF, trading comps and transaction comps
  • Run scenario and sensitivity analysis and build a dashboard
  • Present an investment recommendation with confidence in interviews
Curriculum

Program structure

Module 1 — Excel for Finance ProfessionalsFoundation
  • Modelling layout, colour coding and best practices
  • Modern functions: XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, LET, dynamic arrays
  • Data tables, named ranges and Excel Tables
  • Shortcuts and model-auditing tools
Module 2 — Financial Statement AnalysisFoundation
  • Reading annual reports and notes
  • Ratio analysis and DuPont framework
  • Quality of earnings and red flags
  • Industry benchmarking
Module 3 — Three-Statement Financial ModelCore
  • Assumption sheet and dynamic drivers
  • Revenue and cost build-up
  • Working capital, capex and depreciation schedules
  • Debt schedule, interest and circularity
  • Balancing the balance sheet and error checks
Module 4 — ValuationCore
  • Free cash flow to firm and equity
  • WACC and cost of capital in the Indian context
  • Terminal value: growth and exit multiple
  • Trading and transaction comparables
  • Football-field valuation summary
Module 5 — Advanced TopicsAdvanced
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Introduction to LBO and M&A accretion/dilution
  • Dashboards and charts for decision-makers
  • Using AI tools to speed up modelling responsibly
Capstone — Listed Company ValuationProject
  • End-to-end model on a listed Indian company
  • Investment memo and presentation
  • Model review and feedback
  • Resume project and interview preparation
