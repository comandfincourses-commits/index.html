About the program

What is US CMA?

The Certified Management Accountant (CMA) credential, awarded by the Institute of Management Accountants (IMA), USA, certifies expertise in financial planning, analysis, control, decision support and professional ethics. Each part is a 4-hour computer-based exam combining multiple-choice questions and essay scenarios.

Who should join

  • B.Com, BBA and MBA (Finance) students and graduates
  • CA / CA Inter and CS / CMA (India) aspirants seeking a global credential
  • Accounts, FP&A, costing and MIS professionals
  • Professionals targeting MNCs, GCCs and shared-service centres

What you will be able to do

  • Prepare budgets, forecasts and variance analysis like an FP&A professional
  • Apply costing, performance metrics and internal controls in real businesses
  • Evaluate investments, capital structure and risk using corporate finance tools
  • Write structured, exam-ready answers for CMA essay questions
