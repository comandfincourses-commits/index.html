About the program

What is Claude Training?

A practical program on using Claude productively and responsibly in finance. You will learn how large language models work, how to write effective prompts, and how to use Claude's features — Projects, file analysis, Artifacts and Claude Code — to analyse data, draft reports and automate workflows. Taught by a finance professional, every lab is built around real finance tasks.

Who should join

  • Finance, accounting and audit professionals
  • MBA, BBA and Commerce students
  • FP&A, equity research and credit analysts
  • Corporate teams adopting AI in finance functions

What you will be able to do

  • Write structured prompts that produce reliable, reviewable output
  • Analyse annual reports, earnings calls and contracts with Claude
  • Use Claude to speed up Excel, financial modelling and reporting
  • Design governed AI workflows with proper review and data privacy
Curriculum

Program structure

Module 1 — AI & Claude FundamentalsFoundation
  • How large language models work, and their limits
  • Claude apps, plans and features overview
  • Accuracy, hallucinations and verification habits
  • Data privacy and responsible use in finance
Module 2 — Prompt Engineering for FinanceCore
  • Role, context, task and format prompting
  • Few-shot examples and structured outputs
  • Prompt templates for analysis, summaries and emails
  • Iterating and reviewing AI output
Module 3 — Claude for Financial AnalysisCore
  • Summarising annual reports and earnings calls
  • Ratio analysis and peer comparison with uploaded data
  • Drafting research notes and credit memos
  • Building charts and dashboards with Artifacts
Module 4 — Productivity & AutomationAdvanced
  • Claude with Excel: formulas, models and data cleaning
  • Projects and knowledge bases for your team
  • Introduction to Claude Code and connectors
  • Introduction to the Claude API and AI agents
Capstone — AI-Assisted Finance WorkflowProject
  • Pick a real workflow: reporting, analysis or review
  • Design, build and document the AI-assisted process
  • Controls, review checkpoints and risk assessment
  • Presentation and certification
